SykesLight

A private household calendar display for the Sykes family.

What SykesLight does

SykesLight shows the family's own Google Calendar events on a wall display in our home, and lets a parent add, edit or delete those events from that display. It runs on our own home server.

Who can use it

SykesLight is not offered to the public. It is used only by the owner of this site and members of the household.

Google data

SykesLight requests access to Google Calendar events (the calendar.events scope) only to display and manage the account owner's own calendar events. See the privacy policy.
