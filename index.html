Kitchen Wall

A private household calendar display.

What Kitchen Wall does

Kitchen Wall shows one household's own Google Calendar events on a wall display in the home, and lets a parent add, edit or delete those events from that display. It runs on the household's own home server.

Who can use it

Kitchen Wall is not offered to the public. It is used only by the owner of this site and members of the household.

Google data

Kitchen Wall requests access to Google Calendar events (the calendar.events scope) only to display and manage the account owner's own calendar events. See the privacy policy.
